HOW TO PLAY

🧭 New captain? Start with Expedition Zone 1 — it teaches you as you dive.

The trench is pitch black. Your sonar ping briefly reveals crystals, hunters and the gate — but every ping is noise, and the hunters are blind things that hunt by sound. Ping to see. Ping and be heard.

W/SThrust forward / back (mouse steers, click water to lock pointer)
A/DStrafe

←→↑↓Turn / pitch (when pointer is not locked)

Collect 💎 crystals for coins. Finish the zone mission to open the gate, then swim through it. Hunter core lights: blue = dormant, amber = curious, red = hunting you. Run silent and slow to stay unheard. Earn stars: finish (★), +70% crystals (★★), all crystals (★★★). One golden crystal hides in every zone…
