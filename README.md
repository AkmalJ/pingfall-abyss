# PINGFALL: ABYSS

3D deep-sea sonar-survival game. Built with Three.js; all core 3D models created with Aholo Lux3D for the Manycore Aholo Global 3D AI Hackathon.

## ▶ Play now

**https://akmalj.github.io/pingfall-abyss/**

No install, no login — it runs in your browser. (Diving needs a keyboard & mouse; menus, hangar and the forge work by touch.)

## The game

Your sonar ping is your only light in a lightless trench — and a dinner bell for the blind hunters that hunt by sound.

- **EXPEDITION** — a 10-zone campaign: crystal hunts, rescues, guarded hunter nests, Gate Guardian bosses, 3-star ratings.
- **SURVIVAL** — an endless floor-by-floor descent against THE COLLAPSE, a living ceiling; a Warden boss every 10 floors, a guardian Trio every 50. Three difficulties: CALM (coins ×0.5, no Collapse), STANDARD (×1), NIGHTMARE (×2).
- **ABYSS FORGE** — bring your own Lux3D API key, describe any submarine in plain words, and the game calls the Aholo Lux3D API live, downloads the model, and docks your one-of-one ship in the hangar with real stat trade-offs parsed from your description.

Six submarines, three upgrade tracks, decoys, torpedoes and EMP, one coin economy across both modes, English / 中文 interface.

Controls: WASD + mouse · Space ping · Shift boost · LMB cannon · F torpedo · Q decoy · E EMP · R/C rise & sink · M mute · Esc pause.
